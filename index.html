OVERVIEW

Just tell it what you want to hear.

Classical TTS systems rely on rigid input formats and predefined metadata slots, limiting their ability to fulfill flexible user requirements. Bagpiper-TTS replaces that with a natural language interface: describe any voice, emotion, style, or scenario in plain language, and the model figures out the rest.

Given a free-form request, Bagpiper-TTS first reasons over the user's intent through a structured planning process, then derives a rich caption—a comprehensive textual blueprint encompassing both transcription and nuanced paralinguistic metadata. This caption guides the final speech synthesis.

A single model, five capabilities: classical TTS, multi-talker dialogue, intent-to-speech, character role-play, and singing voice synthesis—all through natural language.
